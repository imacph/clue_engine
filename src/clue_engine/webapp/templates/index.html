How the toolkit score works

Possible-card suggestions count 20 points; probes, ranking, repeat reveals, possible-room preference, and deal analysis each count 15. Card-only suggestion priority counts 10. Applicable options are scaled to 100. Risky accusations are not scored. All bots share deduction and legal movement.

      Actions help
      
            
      Choose one suspect, weapon, and room from the card views, then select Suggest or Accuse. Board suggestions must name the room you occupy; accusations may name any room. Use current room restores it for a suggestion.
      
            
      When responding to a suggestion, select a matching card from your hand to reveal it. If you have no matching card, select Pass.
      
            
      Cards and board rooms can show the viewed player's hand and a positive notebook mark. If a card has multiple positive marks, correct the conflicting notebook entries. Toggle these indicators in Game options.
      
            
      Notebook help
      
            
      Columns show what each player may hold; E is the envelope. A plus means held, a minus means not held, and a question mark means unknown.
      
            
      In a manual notebook, activate any cell to cycle its mark. Hand facts are filled in initially, but you can change them. Use arrow keys to move between cells.
      
            
      To add a clause, select Clause above the notebook, then choose one suspect, weapon, and room in a player column. Choose the envelope column for an incorrect accusation. Select Confirm to save or Cancel to discard.
      
            
      Colored dots identify clauses in each column, including overlapping clauses. Select a dot to open its clause, or select a clause heading in the list to emphasize its linked cells. Select the heading again to clear the selection. Manual clauses can also be edited, resolved, reopened, deleted, or have individual links excluded or restored.
      
            
      Automatic notebooks update deductions and links for you. Manual notes are independent of marks and do not affect bots.
      
            
      The number beside each player in the notebook is their public hand size.
      
            
      Blue marks the turn owner's column, gold marks the player acting now, purple marks players who passed, and green marks players who refuted the current suggestion. The acting player during a private response is highlighted only for its participants; completed passes and refutations are public. Highlighted rows are cards in the current suggestion or accusation; an underlined mark identifies a card visibly shown. These highlights clear or change as play advances.
